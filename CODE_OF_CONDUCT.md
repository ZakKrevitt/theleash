# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the Leash community to be safe, respectful, and productive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and direct.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing experiences and viewpoints.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect private information shared during security or support conversations.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to zak.krevitt@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed privately. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful or unsafe.
